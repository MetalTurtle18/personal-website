This is my "Into to Engineering" project, a device I developed in response to an open-ended prompt to create something to help people.

Welding is a vital skill that allows for many manufacturing possibilities, but the learning curve is steep and requires a lot of time with an expert teacher. In college machine shops, Tungsten Inert Gas (TIG) welding is often taught to those interested in broadening their manufacturing capabilities, as it is the most flexible type of welding. Unfortunately, TIG welding consumes gas, metal, filler, and electricity (up to $100 per hour) during every weld and requires many hours of repetitive practice on potentially dangerous equipment under the supervision of an expert to perfect the proper rhythm for making clean welds.
